Our mission

Creating a platform for responsible mining knowledge.

AIMMS brings together minerals industry professionals and academics to exchange technical knowledge, discuss trends, and define the needs and concerns of Armenia’s mining sector.

Mission in one sentence

To create and foster an Armenian-international mining society that supports better knowledge, better dialogue, and better long-term decisions.

  • Technical knowledge exchange
  • Responsible industry discussion
  • Global-to-local collaboration
  • Support for Armenia’s mining future

AIMMS was founded as a professional bridge between Armenian and international mining and metallurgy communities. The society supports conversations between industry professionals, academics, researchers, and stakeholders who want Armenia’s mining sector to develop with stronger technical standards and more responsible practices.

Through meetings, workshops, technical talks, and professional exchange, AIMMS helps highlight important sector needs, future technologies, environmental challenges, and practical opportunities for improvement.

The redesign presents that mission in a brighter and more accessible format, making the organization feel open, active, and professional.

Mission pillars

What the society stands for.

01

Professional Community

Building a network of Armenian and international specialists connected by mining, metallurgy, and mineral processing.

02

Best Practices

Encouraging modern approaches in technical work, environmental management, process design, and responsible development.

03

Industry Needs

Creating space to identify and discuss the concerns, opportunities, and practical needs of Armenia’s mining sector.

04

Future Technologies

Exploring global trends and innovations that can improve efficiency, safety, transparency, and sustainability.
