Steering Committee

Experienced professionals guiding AIMMS.

Meet the four Steering Committee members bringing expertise in mining, metallurgy, mineral economics, stakeholder relations, and responsible industry development.

Sevan Bedrossian

President

Sevan Bedrossian, M.A.Sc., P.Eng., is a senior hydrometallurgist and technology development leader with over 23 years of international experience in mining, metals, critical minerals, and battery materials. At Hatch, he supports emerging technologies in hydrometallurgy, rare earth processing, and battery recycling. Previously, he led innovation programs at the National Research Council Canada (NRC). His expertise spans engineering design, commissioning, operational optimization, and technology commercialization. He is also an active mentor supporting the next generation of engineers.

Terrence (Srab) Ortslan

Chairman

Terrence (Srab) Ortslan is a mining engineer and mineral economist with extensive operational and management experience across multiple commodities and international jurisdictions. With a background in business studies and international finance, he specializes in mineral policy, mining investment strategies, and industry development. He advocates for independent, ESG-focused professional institutions that promote scientific excellence, responsible resource development, and best practices in geology, engineering, and the mining industry.

Armenia representative

Maggi Esho Nshanian

Vice President · Armenia Representative

Maggi Esho Nshanian is a mining sector professional with more than 20 years of experience in diaspora engagement, stakeholder relations, international cooperation, and industry development. She currently serves as Diaspora Relations administrator at Zangezur Copper Molybdenum Combine (ZCMC), Armenia's leading mining company.
